Reflection

Learning to teach with creativity, purpose, and responsibility

A personal reflection on how Technology for Teaching and Learning shaped my understanding of teaching in a modern classroom.

Learning Technology for Teaching and Learning has helped me realize that teaching is not only about delivering lessons but also about creating meaningful and engaging learning experiences for students. As a future educator, TTL encouraged me to look beyond the big picture by understanding how technology can support different learners, improve classroom interaction, and make lessons more effective and creative. It opened my mind to the importance of innovation, adaptability, and responsible use of digital tools in education.

Through this subject, I learned that technology is not meant to replace teachers, but to strengthen teaching and learning. It taught me how to become more resourceful, patient, and open to new ideas that can help students learn better. I also realized that being a teacher in the 21st century requires continuous learning because technology keeps evolving.

Overall, TTL became a valuable part of my journey as a future educator. It inspired me to become a teacher who is flexible, creative, and committed to helping students succeed in a modern and digital world.
